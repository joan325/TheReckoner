| **February**  | Discovery of Gravitational Waves (LIGO) \+ Picture\! | [http://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/next/physics/big-ideas-of-2016-gravitational-waves-rocked-the-world/](http://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/next/physics/big-ideas-of-2016-gravitational-waves-rocked-the-world/)  | The existence of gravitational waves, as Einstein predicted, is confirmed by the Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory (LIGO). Gravitational waves are infinitesimal disruptions of spacetime that emanate from the motion of matter. The LIGO team detect gravitational waves from the merger of two distant massive black holes. Scientists believe that this discovery could have applications in the proof of String Theory.    |
