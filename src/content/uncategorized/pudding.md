On 12 March 2015, MGCI United Way held a pudding eating competition in the cafeteria at lunch. Students participated in relay teams of four. To participate, students could either sign up as an individual for $2 each, or sign up in a group for $8.  

After the preliminary round, the top three teams competed in the finals. The results were as follows:  

First Place \- AP 16 Crew. (0:20)  

Second Place \- Force to be Reckoned With (0:23)  

Third Place \- \#1 SAC (0:24)  

In total, eleven teams participated in the competition, raising approximately $80-90 for United Way.  

Students who are interested in helping United Way plan future events can join the club on Tuesdays at Lunch in Room 212\.  

Eva Liu, Co-President of MGCI United Way, said that she thought “the competition was a success.” She also mentioned that they “had a lot of fun organizing the event,” and thought that “the contestants had fun too”.
