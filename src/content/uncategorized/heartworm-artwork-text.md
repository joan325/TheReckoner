Heartworm  

*n.* A relationship or friendship that you can’t get out of your head, one which you thought had faded long ago but is still somehow alive and unfinished, like an abandoned campsite whose smoldering embers still have the power to start a forest fire. 

*A forest fire that can destroy everything on the ground.* 

*From space, it’ll look terribly beautiful.*
