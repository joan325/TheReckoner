On 26 May 2016, staff members from _The Reckoner_ attended the Toronto Star High School Newspaper Awards (HSNA) at the Toronto Star Printing Plant. The Reckoner once again won a number of awards, including Best Volunteer Newspaper (with over 15 staff) and Best Electronic Newspaper. Many individuals on _The Reckoner's_ Fifth Guard were recognized for their skills in drawing, photography, opinion and news writing.

At the event, attendees participated in a luncheon and a tour of the Toronto Star printing facility. The tour was considered the highlight of the event by many attendees. According to Marzan Hamid, a News Board writer, “It was amazing to see the real printing process\! The experience definitely gave us insight into how a real newspaper operation is run on a day to day basis.”

The winners of the Toronto Star High School Newspaper Awards can be found below:

Comics (1st Place) – CHRYSTAL ZHANG

Reporting (Runner-up) – RYAN LIN

Editorial Writing (Runner-up) – DAVID HAO

Opinion Writing (1st Place) – KHARI THOMAS

Photography-Sports (Runner-up) – JENNA WONG

Sports Writing-Reporting (Runner-up) – DEIFILIA TO

Layout & Design-Page (1st Place) – ANTHONY CHANG

Electronic-Layout & Design (1st Place) – MICHAEL KIM

Best Electronic Newspaper (1st Place)– MICHAEL KIM

Best Newspaper-Over 15 Staff (1st Place) – THE RECKONER STAFF

The Brad Henderson award is given to a student who demonstrates exemplary leadership and journalistic potential. The winner is awarded $500 and a tour of the _Toronto Star’s_ newsroom. This year’s honour was given to _The Reckoner_’s Editor-in-Chief, David Hao.

_The Reckoner_ won $500 for being named Best Electronic Newspaper, and $500 for being named Best Newspaper-Over 15 Staff. The money will be used to fund _The Reckoner_’s Sixth Guard.

Congratulations to all staff who were recognized at this year’s awards.
