Friends share lovers and  

lovers share friends:   

*Hh, mm, h, hh hh mm.*   

There are all these funny blue green hearts―(here, there, and everywhere)―across   

oh so many of these oh so pale funny grey faded surfaces, you say  

I drew them but I *don’t* remember, *can’t* remember, I forget, all I know:  

This gum tastes like ass, I’ve smushed your birthday  

cake, smeared red and blue, green, white, and also purple  

all around, inside this funny little Ziplock bag. I would never hurt you but I think  

I have, I know  

I will. “Time in any sense,” oh  

*watch me\!* I am oh so clever, watch me smart\! watch me long:  

you pat my head, *it’s up to me*, I’m QUEEN of the world.
